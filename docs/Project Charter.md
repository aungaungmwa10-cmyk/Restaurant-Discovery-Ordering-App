# Project Charter: Restaurant Discovery & Ordering App

**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Target Stage:** MVP (Minimum Viable Product) – Lab 2-3  

---

## Project Overview

The **Restaurant Discovery & Ordering App** is a personalized dining platform that simplifies the process of deciding where and what to eat. By considering the user’s real-time location, budget preferences, peer ratings, and nutritional objectives, the app integrates calorie transparency directly into the browsing and ordering journey. It enables users to make informed, health-conscious choices quickly and effortlessly.

---

## The Problem

Dining decisions often involve juggling multiple, sometimes conflicting, priorities: proximity, affordability, quality, and dietary awareness. Currently, users must toggle between separate platforms to check restaurant ratings, review menus with accurate pricing, and estimate nutritional values. This disjointed experience creates unnecessary friction, making everyday meal planning overwhelming, inefficient, and stressful.

---

## Target Users

- **Health-conscious individuals** – Want to track daily caloric intake without disrupting their dining experience.  
- **Budget-aware students and professionals** – Prefer to filter dining options by clear price tiers (e.g., $, $$, $$$).  
- **Time-constrained users** – Need a fast, reliable way to find nearby food, preview menus, and place orders.  
- **Food explorers** – Enjoy discovering highly rated local restaurants in their immediate area.

---

## Main Features (MVP Scope)

1. **Geolocation-Based Discovery**  
   Automatically identifies the user’s location and displays a curated feed of nearby restaurants.

2. **Rating & Price Filtering**  
   Allows users to refine results by excluding low-rated venues or those outside their specified budget.

3. **Calorie-Aware Menus**  
   Upon selecting a restaurant, users can browse its full menu with total calorie counts clearly displayed next to each item’s price and description.

4. **Seamless Food Ordering**  
   Enables users to add items to a cart, review a comprehensive order summary (including total price and total calories), and complete checkout without hassle.

---

## Future Development (Out of Scope for MVP)

- **AI-Powered Recommendations** – Personalized suggestions based on past orders and flavor preferences.  
- **Advanced Dietary Filters** – Support for allergen and lifestyle tags (e.g., vegan, gluten-free, keto, halal).  
- **Live Delivery Tracking** – Real-time GPS monitoring of delivery progress.  
- **Smart Shopping / Loyalty Program** – Reward mechanisms for healthy eating habits and repeat orders.
