Project Overview

This application delivers a complete restaurant discovery and food ordering solution. Users can find nearby restaurants, explore menus, and place orders through an intuitive interface. The platform integrates key decision-making tools—including ratings, price filters, and nutritional information—to simplify the dining selection process.

Target Users

Health-conscious individuals who monitor their daily nutritional intake

Working professionals needing fast and efficient local food options

Food enthusiasts searching for new dining experiences based on quality and value

Problem Statement

Selecting a restaurant can be overwhelming. Users face multiple challenges, including location uncertainty, budget constraints, health considerations, and quality concerns. The decision process becomes complicated when trying to balance all these factors simultaneously, often leading to frustration and wasted time.

Main Features

Location-Based Discovery – Find restaurants near your current position using GPS technology

Smart Filtering – Sort and refine restaurant results by customer ratings and price levels

Menu Transparency – Access complete menus with clear calorie information for all food items

Order Management – Add items to your shopping cart, track total costs and calories, and complete checkout
